Hi everyone!

I have revamped my website to make it cleaner, faster, and more concise. I replaced the heavy, complex CMS setups with a lightweight **Fetch & Render Markdown** blogging system. Now, writing and sharing my notes is as simple as adding a standard Markdown file to the project.

### Why this design?
I wanted my website to feel like a high-quality journal. By using a warm ivory background, a clean geometric grid, and fine borders, the design focuses entirely on the content. It is structured, readable, and distraction-free.

In this blog, I will share my learnings, project walkthroughs, and notes on topics like:
